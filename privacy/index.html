1. Scope and responsible operator

This Privacy Policy explains how SIPMARK handles personal information in the iPhone app and on its public legal pages. The operator responsible for processing covered by this policy is matt. Contact matt1232026@gmail.com for privacy questions and requests. This policy describes the current SIPMARK implementation. The responsibilities of platform providers for their own services are described in their respective privacy notices.

2. At a glance

Your journal is stored on your iPhone. No SIPMARK account is required and the app has no developer-operated journal sync service. The app does not send drink entries, notes, moods, goals, or Apple Health readings to RevenueCat or a SIPMARK server. Apple and RevenueCat process purchase-related information to provide Pro access. Connecting Apple Health, enabling notifications, and using Lock Screen Quick Log are optional. Opening this policy sends an ordinary web request to GitHub Pages, whose hosting infrastructure receives technical information including your IP address.

3. Journal and setup information

Information you choose to record can include drink types, quantities, volume, alcohol strength, timestamps, alcohol-free confirmations, notes, moods, check-ins, personal goals, and onboarding answers about drinking habits. Local preferences include units, region, reminders, Quick Log presets, optional Health-connection status, and the terms/privacy versions and acceptance time. These records may reveal health-related or otherwise sensitive information. They are processed locally to present your journal, calculate estimates and patterns, and provide the features you request. The operator does not receive your locally stored journal through normal app use.

4. Apple Health information

If you choose to connect Health, SIPMARK requests permission to read alcohol-consumption data and sleep-duration data, and to write the alcohol amounts and times you record. The app uses an installation identifier and entry metadata to manage its own Health samples. Notes, moods, and goals are not written to Health. Read Health values are held separately from the journal and are not included in journal exports. The app does not send Health information to RevenueCat, advertising services, or an operator server, and does not use it for advertising, data brokering, or AI training. System permissions determine actual access. HealthKit does not disclose to the app whether you denied a read permission. Changing access does not erase pre-existing Health samples.

5. Purchase and entitlement information

Apple processes billing and payment details under its own privacy notice. SIPMARK does not receive your full payment-card information. RevenueCat receives purchase and entitlement information needed to retrieve offerings, validate purchases, restore access, and maintain Pro status. This can include an SDK-generated app user identifier, transaction and product identifiers, receipt or purchase records, entitlement status, purchase dates, storefront or locale information, and SDK/device or network metadata associated with service requests. A generated identifier is pseudonymous; it does not make all purchase data anonymous. The app does not intentionally provide RevenueCat with your journal, Health readings, name, email, or advertising identifier. The SDK starts after the current agreements have been accepted. Acknowledging this policy does not constitute advertising consent.

6. Reminders, Live Activities, and exports

Local notifications are scheduled on the device using your selected reminder settings; the app does not operate a remote push-message server for those reminders. Live Activities and Lock Screen Quick Log can display or allow access to selected journal-related information on your Lock Screen. Check privacy options before using a shared device. Exports are created locally and leave the app only when you choose a destination in the system share sheet. A recipient, email app, cloud drive, or other destination then handles the exported file under its own rules. Exported journal files may contain sensitive information. Review and protect them before sharing.

7. Support and technical information

If you contact the operator voluntarily, it receives the contact details, message, attachments, and diagnostic information you choose to send. Do not send your full journal or Health data unless needed and explicitly requested for a specific support purpose. The current app contains no general product-analytics or advertising SDK and no operator-run journal backend. Apple may provide developer diagnostics or crash information according to your system sharing settings; such reports may contain device, operating-system, and error information. They are used to address defects and security issues rather than profile your alcohol use.

8. Legal-page hosting and cookies

The legal pages use static HTML and local styles, without added analytics, advertising pixels, external fonts, or operator-set tracking cookies. GitHub hosts the pages. GitHub states that visitors’ IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes even when visitors are not signed in. Hosting requests may also expose browser/network request metadata. GitHub’s own privacy notice applies to its hosting operations. Visiting these pages does not upload your journal or Health data. External links, if opened, are governed by the destination’s notices.

9. Purposes and legal bases for EEA and UK users

Where EU or UK data protection law applies, purchase and entitlement processing necessary to deliver requested Pro access is based on performance of a contract. Necessary service security and proportionate technical troubleshooting may be based on legitimate interests in maintaining a reliable app, balanced against your rights. Mandatory financial or legal records are processed to comply with legal obligations. Optional permissions rely on your affirmative choice; where operator processing of special-category health information requires explicit consent, it must be obtained separately and specifically before that processing occurs. Local journal and Health operations do not authorize remote collection by the operator. A generic Agree & Continue action is not a substitute for explicit health-data consent. You may withdraw optional consent or permissions prospectively without affecting prior lawful processing. Declining optional features does not prevent access to the remaining app, subject to its disclosed purchase requirements.

10. Who receives information

Recipients are limited to parties involved in the feature you use: Apple for payments, platform services, Health, and optional device backups; RevenueCat for purchase and entitlement management; GitHub for legal-page hosting; and a recipient you choose for an export. The operator may receive support messages and authorized diagnostic reports. Service providers must be governed by appropriate terms and safeguards for any information processed on the operator’s behalf. Information may be disclosed when legally required, to protect legitimate rights or safety, or in a lawful business transfer with appropriate notice and safeguards. Such circumstances do not create access to a journal that has never been uploaded. We do not make your journal public.

11. No sale, targeted advertising, or health profiling

The current app does not sell personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use personal or Health information for targeted advertising. It does not access the advertising identifier or run advertising attribution integrations. It does not use journal or Health information to train AI models or make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects. Local summaries and goal comparisons are descriptive features rather than eligibility, employment, insurance, or medical decisions. If practices change, disclosures and any required consent or opt-out mechanisms must be updated before the new processing begins.

12. Storage, retention, and deletion

The journal is stored in an atomic file-protected archive in Application Support. Local preferences and some installation-related metadata are stored using iOS storage facilities. Local records remain until you remove or change them, delete the local journal, or remove the app, subject to system backup and restore behavior. The operator has no server copy of your journal to delete. Health data remains in Apple Health until removed there; deleting the local journal does not delete Health records. Exported files and backups remain under the control of their destination or backup provider. Purchase records are retained only as needed to manage entitlements, prevent fraud, meet legal/accounting requirements, and resolve disputes, subject to vendor requirements. Support records should be retained only as necessary to resolve requests and meet applicable obligations. Retention is determined by whether the information remains necessary for its stated purpose, applicable legal requirements, pending disputes, and provider obligations. Records are deleted or de-identified when no longer necessary, subject to lawful exceptions. Contact us to request information about retention or deletion of a specific record.

13. Your practical controls

You can edit journal entries, export your journal, and delete local journal data in Settings without Pro. You can turn off reminders or revoke notification permission in iOS Settings. You can disable the Health connection and manage Health permissions in Apple Health. Review Health separately to remove records already stored there. Manage Live Activities, device backups, and exported files using the relevant platform controls. Deleting app data does not cancel an Apple subscription or erase Apple/RevenueCat transaction records. Cancel subscriptions through Apple’s subscriptions management page. For a purchase-data or support-record request, contact matt1232026@gmail.com; additional vendor action may be necessary.

14. EEA, UK, and Swiss privacy rights

Subject to applicable law and exceptions, you may request access to personal data held by the operator, correction, erasure, restriction, and portability; object to processing based on legitimate interests; and withdraw consent. You may complain to your competent data protection authority and need not contact us first. Requests under EU/UK law are generally answered within one month; permitted extensions must be explained within that period. We may verify identity proportionately but will not ask for an unnecessary copy of your full journal. We cannot disclose or retrieve local-only data we do not possess. Rights relating to data held independently by Apple or GitHub may need to be exercised with those providers. Send requests to matt1232026@gmail.com with the subject “Privacy request.”

Where applicable state law covers the operator and processing, rights may include knowing/accessing categories and specific information, obtaining a portable copy, correcting inaccuracies, deleting data, opting out of sale, targeted advertising or certain profiling, and limiting specified uses of sensitive information. Sources, purposes, and recipients are described above. We do not sell or share information for cross-context advertising and do not process it for targeted advertising or covered consequential profiling. You will not be discriminated against for exercising applicable rights. Authorized agents may submit requests with proportionate authorization checks. Where required, requests are generally answered within 45 days, with lawful extensions and appeal rights; an appeal can be submitted by emailing matt1232026@gmail.com with the subject “Privacy appeal”. If consumer-health statutes such as Washington or Nevada law apply to operator-held information, collection, sharing, consent, withdrawal, deletion, and authorized recipients must satisfy those laws. This policy does not authorize selling consumer health data or bypass separate consent requirements. Purchase, support, and hosting information may be processed outside your country, including in the United States, by the relevant providers. Where the operator transfers data protected by EU/UK/Swiss law, it must use an applicable lawful mechanism, such as an adequate destination, a verified applicable certification, or approved contractual safeguards with any necessary supplementary measures. Contact matt1232026@gmail.com to ask about a specific recipient, processing location, or applicable transfer safeguard. Any reliance on a certification or contractual safeguard must apply to the actual transfer concerned.

17. Security and children

We use the protections implemented by iOS and file-protected local storage, and limit remote processing to the services described here. No storage or transmission method is perfectly secure. Use a device passcode, keep software current, and protect exports and backups. The app does not use HIPAA compliance as a marketing claim and is not a replacement for a healthcare provider’s secure medical-record system. SIPMARK is intended for adults 18 and older and is not directed to children. If you believe a child has provided information to the operator, email matt1232026@gmail.com so it can investigate and address information it actually holds; local data can be removed on the device.
